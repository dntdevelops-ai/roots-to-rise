Roots · Honoring the past

Once known as Colored Town.

East Thomaston is home to a historic African American community known as Colored Town. For about 160 years, families here have gathered for one of the longest continuous Emancipation celebrations in Georgia.

Reverend McGill has researched this history since the 1970s and wrote a book on its first 100 years. Dr. Dawson's team at Georgia Southern University has documented the community's Emancipation story. On August 9, 2026, 64 residents came together to cast a vision for its future, and they named it the Guilford Cunningham Historic District.

A filmmaker recorded those community identity interviews: residents, elders, and descendants telling the story in their own words. Roots to Rise exists because the people who live in a community should shape what is built there.

Ground · Progress you can stand on

We already own the ground.

The 7.83 acres came from a longtime East Thomaston family Confirm wording. The Thomaston Housing Authority wrote the largest check toward the purchase, because it wants to help families move from rental units into homes of their own. The Peoples Bank of Georgia financed the land.

Since then the City of Thomaston has approved the rezoning. University partners, the Sarah B placemaking team, and Atwell Engineering are now helping shape the plan. Founding funders gave before anyone knew what the project would need.

Rise · Bridging the future

33 homes. Owned, not rented.

Phase I plans 24 single-family homes and 9 townhomes for sale on the 7.83-acre site, aimed at families earning 80% to 120% of area median income: teachers, nurses, first responders, and the grandchildren of Colored Town, building generational wealth where their roots are.

Pricing is subject to financial validation. Homes are not available for purchase at this time.

Phase II, now in planning and partnership development, adds what a neighborhood needs to thrive: Growing food. Cultivating talent. Creating opportunity.

Questions

Does my gift buy or reserve a home?

No. Your gift funds the groundwork for the whole development. It does not purchase a home, reserve a home, or provide a financial return. Homes are not available for purchase at this time, and final pricing is subject to financial validation.

What exactly does the $150,000 pay for?

Predevelopment: land surveys and easement due diligence, civil and site engineering, infrastructure and utility planning, architectural modifications to approved home designs, and development coordination with owner's representative support.

How do pledges work?

Record your amount and the date you'll give, any day through December 31, 2026. You'll get a written pledge acknowledgment. The tracker counts gifts once they're received; pledges are tracked separately until fulfilled.

How does the match work?

Gifts to The Bridge Solution, Inc. are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and every gift receives an emailed receipt for your records Confirm 501(c)(3) status and add EIN

Can I choose where my gift goes?

Yes. Like an offering envelope, the giving form lets you choose Roots to Rise groundwork, the Guilford Cunningham Historic District designation, or wherever it's needed most. We honor your choice, and we direct funds where they're needed most.
